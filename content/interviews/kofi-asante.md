**Howdy, who are you?**

I am Kofi, I work at Arup as a structural engineer in Accra and I have been here for about 3 years now. Before that I did my NSS at a construction firm in Tema and honestly that year taught me more than I expected.

**What did you study and where?**

Civil Engineering at KNUST. Graduated 2019. It was a journey — 6 years that felt like 60 at some points but here we are.

**How did your course get you to where you are now?**

Not a straight line at all. I thought I was going to be designing roads but I ended up in structural — buildings, bridges, that kind of thing. The fundamentals from school are still very relevant though. Statics, soil mechanics, all of it comes back when you are on site.

**What do you use daily to get the job done?**

AutoCAD is life. I also use STAAD Pro for structural analysis and a lot of Excel honestly — nobody tells you how much engineering is just spreadsheets. My HP laptop has been through war and is still standing.

**What would you tell someone in Civil Engineering right now?**

Get your hands dirty early. Do not wait till after school to touch a real project. And please learn software on your own — school will not teach you AutoCAD properly, YouTube will.
