**Who are you?**

I am Ama, a final year Computer Science student at KNUST. People know me as the girl who is always debugging something at 2am. I am from Kumasi and I chose CS because I wanted to build things that actually work and actually matter.

**Why did you choose this course?**

Honestly I thought it was going to be all about coding and building apps. I was wrong. The maths almost took me out in level 200 — Discrete Mathematics, Linear Algebra, all of it. But I survived and I am glad I stayed. The coding part came eventually and it was worth it.

**What do you use to learn?**

I use YouTube a lot — channels like Fireship and Traversy Media. For the theory stuff I use my lecture notes and past questions, that is the real weapon at KNUST. I also use ChatGPT to explain concepts when I am stuck and VS Code is my editor for everything.

**How is it going so far?**

It has been tough but rewarding. Level 200 was the hardest — the maths is no joke. By level 300 things started clicking and I started actually enjoying it. Final year projects are stressful but I am building something I am proud of.

**What would you tell someone considering this course?**

Do not come in thinking it is just about coding. The maths and theory are real and they will test you. But if you are curious and you like solving problems you will enjoy it. Also find your people early — group study saved my life more than once.
