Howdy! I am Peter Prince but people call me kwabsntim or kwabs for short. I built this site and I run it. If you want to help me maintain it, I am very open to that. But first read my interview below and then we can talk.

---

**Who are you?**

I am prince, an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate from UMaT. Just graduated this year and currently waiting for my NSS postings, so yes, I am in that in-between phase.

**Why did you choose your course?**

Well the reason is quite funny. I wanted something challenging. In Ghana, medicine and engineering are known as the hardest paths, and I knew medicine wasn't for me. But beyond the challenge, I have had this passion for building things since childhood and being curious. So engineering was the obvious path. It just made sense.

**What do you use to learn?**

My HP 840 G1 EliteBook goes everywhere with me. VSCode is my go-to because I write more code than I study. VLC for movies and spotify for melodies, cos you need balance. And the AIs. ChatGPT and Claude are my most used tools right now. I use them a lot.And for social media you will find me everywhere but annonymously except whatsapp.

**How did your course go?**

Engineering is not easy chale!, I will say that plainly. The stress, the maths, the sleepless nights is all real. But I have graduated, and that feels good. You will learn a lot if you are willing to go through it. The course delivers on what it promises. But broo! it is just not for the faint-hearted.

**What would you tell someone considering this course?**

Be mentally and physically strong. That is not a joke. But if you have a genuine passion for building things and you are willing to push through the hard parts, you will come out on the other side with something real. Just go in with your eyes open.
